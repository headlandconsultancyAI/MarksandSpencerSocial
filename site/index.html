How this is worked out
  • Data is scraped from each leader's own LinkedIn, Instagram and TikTok profiles, covering the last 12 months. It refreshes every morning at 7.30.
  • Kathryn Turner's TikTok account started on 14 May 2026, so her TikTok numbers cover about six months.
  • Posts made by other accounts that appear on a leader's profile, such as collaborations with M&S News or the Cabinet Office, are left out. Leaders' own reposts with commentary are kept.
  • Engagements add up likes or reactions, comments and shares. TikTok saves are included. Instagram shares and LinkedIn views aren't in the data.
  • One Instagram post has its likes hidden, so only its comments count.
  • Topics are tagged from each post's words against a fixed list. A post goes to the topic it mentions most.

        How this is worked out
        • Covers the M&S corporate LinkedIn page, @marksandspencernews on Instagram and @MandSnews on X over the last 12 months. The consumer @marksandspencer accounts aren't included.
        • Posts by other accounts that show up on these feeds, such as a Cabinet Office post on the LinkedIn page, are left out. The company page's own reposts are kept.
        • Engagements add up likes or reactions, comments and shares. On X, shares are reposts plus quotes. Views aren't counted, and Instagram doesn't give shares.
        • Each post gets one theme from the words it uses. A post naming an MP, MSP or minister goes to Public affairs. A handful of posts with no caption or no clear theme are left out of the theme charts.
        • The "times a typical post" figure compares a theme's engagements with what the same number of average posts on the same channels would get. It stops LinkedIn's bigger numbers drowning out X. A single big post, like the Christmas ad, can lift a small theme a long way.
        • Follower numbers aren't in the LinkedIn and Instagram data, so engagement rates aren't shown.

          How this is worked out
          • Brand channels are @marksandspencer and @marksandspencerfood on Instagram. Style and Man aren't included. The window is the last 12 months.           • A story is matched from the words in a post, against a fixed list of 20. A post can carry more than one story.
